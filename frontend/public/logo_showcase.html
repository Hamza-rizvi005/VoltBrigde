Option 1

The Solar Arch

Sun + Suspension Bridge

Literally depicts the bridge between daytime solar production and nighttime/power-cut coverage. Warm golden sun rays anchored by a strong green suspension arch.

Key Feeling: Friendly, warm, intuitive.
Target Audience: Families & general homeowners.
Option 2

The Hex-Shield

Solar Cells + Battery Fortress

Isometric hexagonal cell representing an energy store and home protection. Upper facet represents solar panels; lower base represents protected battery reserve tiers.

Key Feeling: Robust, protective, engineered.
Target Audience: Tech competitions & IoT demos.
Option 3

The Infinite V-Bridge

Typographic V + Flow Loop

Modern clean-tech monogram where a sharp 'V' (Volt) connects into an arching suspension curve forming an infinite continuous energy cycle.

Key Feeling: Sleek, minimalist, digital SaaS.
Target Audience: Product designers & modern app users.

How to Choose:

Select Option 1 if you want the strongest metaphor for the project pitch ("We bridge the solar day to the night blackout").
Select Option 2 if you want to emphasize battery protection, engineering robustness, and hardware-in-the-loop safety.
Select Option 3 if you want a trendy, minimalist tech startup brand (like Linear or Tesla Energy).
