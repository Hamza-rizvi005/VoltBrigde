## Assistant brief

Volt Bridge is a household solar energy planning platform for homes with limited battery storage and frequent power cuts. It helps users understand when to use appliances, preserve backup power, and plan extra consumption for occasions such as a gathering at home.

Users enter their solar panel, inverter, battery, and appliance details, along with expected power cuts. The app presents an easy daily energy plan, estimated battery backup, suggested appliance schedules, actionable alerts, and a plain-English AI assistant. Live solar data can be connected where supported.

Build a polished, responsive frontend with a dark theme, distinctive animations, and relevant 3D solar and battery visuals. Include a detailed public landing page, registration/login, guided household setup, dashboard, daily planner, appliance management, power-cut planning, event planning, and AI chat. Keep the interface simple for nontechnical household users.
